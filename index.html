Mr. Habimana 👨🏾‍🔬
Sometimes bats carry tiny germs called viruses. When germs pass from animals to people, it's called a zoonotic disease. But with simple rules, we stay safe! ✅
🚫 RULE 1: Never Touch Bats!
🧼 RULE 2
CLEAN!
Wash Your Hands! 🧼

After touching anything near bat areas — wash hands with soap and water for 20 seconds! This stops ALL germs from spreading! 💧
🙌🧼💧
🍎 RULE 3: Don't Eat Bat-Touched Fruit!
🫙 RULE 4
Cover Food and Water! 🫙

Always keep food and water in covered containers — especially at night when bats are active! Bat droppings in uncovered food is dangerous! 🌙
🥘✅ 💧✅
🏥 RULE 5: Get Medical Help Immediately!

CHAPTER 3
Dead Bats & Bats in Your Home!
❓ Mutesi's Question!
Mutesi 👧🏽
Sometimes we find dead bats near our homes. What should we do?! 😟
☠️ How to Handle Dead Bats
1
Wear Gloves! Use thick gloves or plastic bags over your hands. NEVER bare skin! 🧤
2
Pick Up Carefully! Place the bat in a bag. Don't squeeze it. Keep fluids away from your face. 🛍️
3
Bury or Burn! Bury at least half a metre deep away from water and children's play areas. 🪦
4
Wash Hands! After disposal — soap and water immediately! 🧼
⚠️ VERY IMPORTANT!
STOP!
🚫👦🏾🦇
Children should NEVER handle dead bats!

Always ask an adult to do this safely.

If anyone is scratched while handling a dead bat — seek medical help IMMEDIATELY! 🏥
🦇 A Bat Flew Into Your Home! What Do You Do?
✅ DO THIS!
✅Open all windows and doors to let the bat fly out on its own! 🪟
✅Turn off lights inside and turn on a light outside to guide the bat out! 💡
✅Stay calm! Keep children and pets away until the bat leaves. 🐈
❌ NOT THIS!
❌Do NOT try to catch the bat with your hands! 🙅
❌Do NOT throw objects at it or swing at it! 🪃
❌Do NOT panic! The bat is more afraid of you! 😱
🏠 Bats Living in Your Roof? Here's What to Do!
👁️
Watch at dusk to see where bats enter and exit — look for gaps in the roof! 🌇
🔨
Seal gaps with wood or mesh ONLY after bats leave at night — never seal them inside! 🚫
🧤
Clean droppings wearing gloves and a face cloth. Wet them first to reduce dust! 💧
📞
Call for help if the colony is large! Contact local wildlife or health authorities. 🦺

CHAPTER 4
Outdoor Safety & Bat Droppings!
🌿 Keep Your Outdoor Space Safe!
🧹
Clean droppings from surfaces near your home regularly. Wear gloves and a face covering! 🧤
🪣
Cover water tanks and rain-collection containers so bat droppings cannot contaminate your drinking water! 💧
🐟
Cover food drying areas at night when bats are active — fish, grain, and other foods! 🌙
🦇
Never use poison to remove bats — it harms the environment and is often illegal! 🚫
⚠️ About Bat Droppings (Guano)!
😷
Wear a face mask or damp cloth over nose and mouth near guano
💧
Wet the droppings with water before sweeping to prevent dust flying up
🧤
Always wear gloves and long sleeves when cleaning guano
🧒🏾
Children should NEVER play in areas where guano has collected!
🪨 Visiting Bat Caves? Stay Safe!
Before entering any bat cave: Put on a face mask (N95 if possible), gloves, long sleeves, long trousers, and closed shoes. Stay as short a time as possible inside!
🤫
Move slowly and quietly — don't disturb roosting bats!
🙌
Avoid touching cave walls — they are coated with guano and urine!
👕
Change clothes and shower as soon as possible after visiting!
🏥
Feeling unwell after a cave visit? See a health worker and tell them you visited a bat cave!

CHAPTER 5
Protect Bats — Protect Ourselves!
🌍 Why We Must Protect Bats!
🤝
Bats Are NOT Our Enemies! They do not try to make us sick. Disease happens mostly when humans destroy bat habitats.
⚠️
Killing Bats Makes Things Worse! Scared bats scatter and spread germs further — and we lose their pest control and pollination benefits!
🌳
Protect Their Homes! Protecting forests, caves, and wetlands where bats live is the best way to prevent diseases from spreading to people.
🦸 What YOU Can Do!
🌳
Do NOT cut down roost trees — bats need them!
🪨
Do NOT throw stones at bats — they are protected!
📢
Tell others the FACTS about bats — share what you have learned today!
🏠
Build bat houses far from your home to give bats a roost of their own!
📞
Report sick or dead bats to authorities — never ignore them!
🦇 OUR PROMISE TO BATS! 🦇
☑️
We will NEVER touch or disturb wild bats.
☑️
We will protect the trees, caves, and forests where bats live.
☑️
We will cover our food and water to keep them clean.
☑️
We will wash our hands often, especially near wildlife.
☑️
We will tell a grown-up if we find a sick, injured, or dead bat.
☑️
We will see a health worker immediately if a bat bites or scratches us.
☑️
We will share the truth about bats and help others understand.
☑️
We remember: healthy bats = a healthy environment for ALL! 🌍

Amazing Bat Facts! Did You Know?
🌍
There are more than 1,400 species of bats worldwide — living on every continent except Antarctica! 🧊
✈️
Bats are the only mammals capable of true powered flight! A bat's wings are actually elongated hand bones with skin stretched between the fingers. 🖐️
🦟
Insect-eating bats can consume more than 1,000 mosquitoes in a single hour! 🦟🦟🦟
🍌
Bats pollinate over 300 species of fruit plants and spread seeds of more than 500 plant species. 🌱
🔊
Echolocation allows many bats to navigate and hunt in complete darkness using sound waves — like a natural sonar system! 📡
👁️
Most bats have good eyesight — the saying "blind as a bat" is a total MYTH! 🙅 Bats can see perfectly well!
👶🏾
Mother bats can identify their own baby among thousands of pups by its unique call and scent! 🥰
🎂
Some bats can live over 30 years — extraordinarily long for small mammals! 🎉
💰
Bats save farmers billions of dollars every year in reduced crop damage from insects! 🌾🌾
🏠
When bat habitats are destroyed, bats are forced closer to human settlements — increasing disease risk! So protecting bat habitats protects US! 🌳
